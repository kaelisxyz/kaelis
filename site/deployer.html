Method

How to read a record.

What countsOnly tokens Kaelis has tested. A wallet may have launched others that Kaelis never saw. Every number on this page has that limit.
The deployerThe token's creator from RugCheck's report. If that is unavailable, the fee payer of the oldest transaction on the mint, when the history is short enough to reach it.
The funderThe source of the transfer in the wallet's first transaction. A funder with 1,000 or more transactions is treated as a hub (an exchange, a bridge, a bot service) and never forms a cluster, because thousands of unrelated people share it.
The clusterTwo or more deployers whose first funds came from the same wallet that is not a hub. On pump.fun most launches come from a fresh wallet, so repeat launchers usually show up here and not in one wallet's history.
RiskThe share of the wallet's tested tokens expected to end as rugs. A confirmed rug counts 1, a token that held counts 0, an open call counts its published rug chance. A weak prior (0.3 over one token) keeps a single launch from being a verdict.
LevelsSerial: three confirmed rugs, or two that are at least half of the resolved tokens. Flagged: one confirmed rug. Clean: two or more tokens held, none rugged. Watch: no result yet and risk of 50 or more. New: no result yet.
OutcomesHeld or rugged comes from the Ledger: the same hash-chained calls, checked 7 days after each one.
Not for saleKaelis does not sell ratings. Nobody can pay to change a record. A record is a risk estimate from public data, not an accusation and not financial advice.
