The loop

Patrol, call, check, evolve.

01 · PatrolScan on its own

Every 30 minutes Kaelis scans new Solana tokens by itself. Nobody has to ask.

02 · CallSeal a prediction

Each scan becomes a hash-chained call on the Ledger, with every check value sealed in.

03 · CheckSee what happened

Seven days later it reads the pools again: rugged or held.

04 · EvolveBreed a better rule set

Mutants are scored on calls they never trained on. Only a winner becomes the next generation.

    Guardrails

    Smarter, never sneakier.

    HoldoutAbout 30% of tokens are set aside forever by a fixed hash. A new generation is judged only on those, so it can't just memorise the past.
    BoundsEach weight stays between 2 and 40, the weights always add up to 100, and the sell test can never drop below 15.
    ProofEach generation records its parent, its changes and its before and after IQ, and is hash-chained to the one before.
    Minimum dataNo evolution until there are enough resolved calls. Until then, generation 0 runs.
    Not adviceA smarter rule set is still a risk estimate, never a buy or sell signal.
