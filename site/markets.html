An official tokenized stock is a claim on a share held by a regulated issuer, so the issuer keeps legal controls: it can mint, freeze and move tokens. Kaelis lists those controls apart instead of counting them as a rug signal. You are trusting the issuer, not the code. The grade covers the market checks (selling back, liquidity, pool age) and stops at A.
