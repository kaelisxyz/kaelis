How to use Kaelis.

Kaelis does three things. Here is what each one does and how you use it.

Demo with example data. Nothing here is financial advice.
01 · Scan a token

Check a token before you buy.

Kaelis looks for seven known scam signs, like liquidity that can be pulled or a sell that fails, and gives a risk score.

  1. Copy the token's address.
  2. Send it to the Kaelis bot on Telegram or X.
  3. Get a score from 0 to 100, with proof for every warning.
0–39 low40–69 suspicious70+ dangerous

A low score means no known red flag was found. It is not a promise.

02 · Build

Earn by shipping real code.

Proof-of-Build rewards merged work in public code repos, not hype. Your work becomes a tower in Forge City.

  1. Link your code account (read-only) and sign once with your wallet.
  2. Ship. Only merged work in public repos counts.
  3. Each week, claim your share. Your tower gains a floor.

Spam commits and copy-paste are filtered out.

03 · Hold

Light a window in the city.

Every wallet holding $KAEL lights one window in Forge City. Your window stays in the same place every visit.

  1. Hold $KAEL in your wallet.
  2. Your window lights up within a few minutes.
  3. Sell everything and it turns off.

No sign-up. Kaelis only reads public balances.

How each part works.

Fig. 01 — Kaelis Core pipelineEach stage is logged and replayable
  1. 01
    Resolve the token

    Paste a mint address or ticker. Kaelis finds the mint, its pools and its creator wallet.

  2. 02
    Run seven checks

    Authorities, liquidity lock, holder concentration, developer history, a simulated sell and funding clusters. Each check returns a severity from 0 to 1 with the transactions that prove it.

  3. 03
    Score 0 to 100

    Weights add up to 100. A failed sell simulation is a hard flag that forces the score to at least 85.

  4. 04
    Keep watching

    Scanned tokens are re-checked. If over half the liquidity leaves in one transaction, or the developer dumps, Kaelis marks a rug and the building falls in Forge City.

  1. 01
    Link your code account and a wallet

    Read-only sign-in to your code account, plus a signed message from your wallet. One code account maps to one wallet.

  2. 02
    Verify real work

    Only commits in public repos count, and only once they land in a merged pull request or on the default branch. Forks, generated files and copy-paste commits are filtered out.

  3. 03
    Score the epoch

    Each week, build points come from merged changes, review activity and how many other people use the repo. A daily cap stops anyone from farming points with tiny commits.

  4. 04
    Attest and reward

    A hash of the weekly results is posted on-chain. Builders claim their share of the Proof-of-Build pool with a Merkle proof, and their tower grows a floor.

  1. 01
    Snapshot holders

    An indexer reads every $KAEL token account every few minutes.

  2. 02
    Filter dust

    Wallets under a minimum balance are ignored, so nobody can light a thousand windows with a thousand empty wallets.

  3. 03
    Assign a window

    Your window position comes from a hash of your wallet address. It stays the same every time you come back.

  4. 04
    Light or dim

    Holding lights your window. Selling everything turns it off at the next snapshot.

  1. 01
    Event-driven

    New pools, large transfers and code pushes arrive as events. Kaelis acts on them in the order they happened.

  2. 02
    Rules decide, the model explains

    Scores come only from the open rules. The language model gets the evidence and writes the summary, and it cannot change a number.

  3. 03
    Replayable

    Every input is stored with its block height. Anyone can re-run a score from the public rules and get the same result.

  4. 04
    Speaks where people are

    The same results drive Forge City, a Telegram and X bot, and a public API.
