Kaelis is an agent that checks crypto tokens for scams and rewards people who actually build. Here is how it works, in plain words. Kaelis adalah agent yang ngecek token crypto dari scam, dan ngasih reward ke orang yang beneran build. Ini cara kerjanya, pake bahasa simpel.

Status: demo with example data. Nothing here is financial advice.Status: demo pakai data contoh. Bukan saran keuangan.
01 · The problem01 · Masalahnya

New tokens appear every minute. Most people can't tell which ones are traps.Token baru muncul tiap menit. Kebanyakan orang gak bisa bedain mana yang jebakan.

RugThe creator pulls the money out of the trading pool and the price drops to almost zero in one move.Pembuat token narik uang dari pool trading, harga langsung jatuh hampir nol dalam satu transaksi.
HoneypotYou can buy the token but you can't sell it, or selling costs you most of what you put in.Lu bisa beli tokennya tapi gak bisa jual, atau pas jual hampir semua uang lu kepotong.
Unpaid buildersBuilder gak dibayarThe people writing real code rarely get rewarded, while hype gets all the attention.Orang yang nulis kode beneran jarang dapet reward, yang dapet perhatian malah hype.
02 · What Kaelis does02 · Apa yang Kaelis lakuin

One agent, three jobs.Satu agent, tiga tugas.

Scam scanner

Checks a token before you buyNgecek token sebelum lu beli

Paste a token address. Kaelis runs seven checks and gives a risk score from 0 to 100, with proof for every warning.Tempel alamat token. Kaelis jalanin tujuh pengecekan dan kasih skor risiko 0 sampai 100, lengkap sama bukti tiap peringatan.

Example: "82 / dangerous: liquidity not locked"Contoh: "82 / bahaya: likuiditas gak dikunci"

Proof-of-Build

Rewards real codeReward buat kode beneran

Builders link GitHub and a wallet. Each week, merged work on public repos earns build points and a share of a reward pool.Builder sambungin GitHub sama wallet. Tiap minggu, kerjaan yang di-merge di repo publik dapet poin build dan bagian dari reward pool.

Example: 12 merged PRs this week → tower grows 1 floorContoh: 12 PR di-merge minggu ini → tower naik 1 lantai

Holder light

Holders light up the cityHolder nyalain kota

Every wallet holding $KAEL lights one window in Forge City. Sell everything and your window turns off.Tiap wallet yang pegang $KAEL nyalain satu jendela di Forge City. Jual semua, jendela lu mati.

Example: 4,120 holders → 4,120 lit windowsContoh: 4.120 holder → 4.120 jendela nyala

03 · How a scan works03 · Cara scan bekerja

From address to answer in four steps.Dari alamat ke jawaban dalam empat langkah.

  1. ReadBacaKaelis reads public data about the token from the blockchain: who created it, who holds it, where the money sits.Kaelis baca data publik token dari blockchain: siapa yang bikin, siapa yang pegang, uangnya disimpen di mana.
  2. CheckCekSeven fixed checks look for known scam patterns, such as a test sell that fails or a creator holding a huge share.Tujuh pengecekan tetap nyari pola scam yang udah dikenal, misalnya tes jual yang gagal atau pembuat yang pegang porsi gede banget.
  3. ScoreSkorEach check has a weight. Kaelis adds them up into a score from 0 to 100. The same data always gives the same score.Tiap cek punya bobot. Kaelis jumlahin jadi skor 0 sampai 100. Data yang sama selalu kasih skor yang sama.
  4. ExplainJelasinAn AI writes a short summary in plain words. It reads the result but can never change the score.AI nulis ringkasan singkat pake bahasa biasa. AI cuma baca hasilnya, gak bisa ngubah skornya.

04 · Why you can check it04 · Kenapa bisa dipercaya

Rules decide. AI only explains.Aturan yang mutusin. AI cuma ngejelasin.

Many "AI scanners" are a black box. Kaelis is built so anyone can check the work.Banyak "AI scanner" itu kotak hitam. Kaelis dibikin supaya siapa aja bisa ngecek kerjaannya.

01

Open rulesAturan terbuka

Every check and every weight will be published on GitHub.Semua cek dan bobotnya bakal dipublish di GitHub.

02

Proof for every flagBukti tiap peringatan

Each warning links to the exact transactions or wallets behind it.Tiap peringatan ada link ke transaksi atau wallet yang jadi buktinya.

03

Same input, same scoreInput sama, skor sama

Every score records the data and rule version used, so anyone can re-run it and get the same number.Tiap skor nyimpen data dan versi aturan yang dipake, jadi siapa aja bisa ulang dan dapet angka yang sama.

04

Public data onlyCuma data publik

Kaelis reads the blockchain and GitHub. It never asks for your keys, your funds or trading access.Kaelis cuma baca blockchain dan GitHub. Gak pernah minta private key, dana, atau akses trading lu.

05 · Proof-of-Build05 · Proof-of-Build

Write code, grow a tower.Nulis kode, tower lu tumbuh.

Rewards go to work other people can see and use, not to whoever makes the most noise.Reward buat kerjaan yang bisa dilihat dan dipake orang lain, bukan buat yang paling rame.

  1. LinkSambunginConnect GitHub (read-only) and sign a message with your wallet. One GitHub account, one wallet.Sambungin GitHub (cuma baca) dan tanda tangan pesan pake wallet. Satu akun GitHub, satu wallet.
  2. BuildBuildOnly merged work on public repos counts. Copy-paste and tiny spam commits are filtered out.Cuma kerjaan yang di-merge di repo publik yang dihitung. Copy-paste dan commit spam kecil-kecil difilter.
  3. Score the weekHitung per mingguPoints come from merged changes, reviews and real usage of the repo, with caps so nobody can farm it.Poin dari perubahan yang di-merge, review, dan pemakaian repo yang beneran, ada batasnya biar gak bisa di-farming.
  4. ClaimKlaimThe weekly result is posted on-chain. Builders claim their share, and their tower in Forge City gains a floor.Hasil mingguan diposting on-chain. Builder klaim bagiannya, dan tower mereka di Forge City nambah satu lantai.

08 · FAQ

Questions people ask.Pertanyaan yang sering muncul.

Does Kaelis tell me what to buy?Kaelis nyuruh gua beli apa?

No. Kaelis reports risk. It never predicts prices or promises profit. A low score only means no known red flag was found.Nggak. Kaelis cuma ngelaporin risiko. Gak pernah nebak harga atau janji profit. Skor rendah cuma artinya gak ketemu tanda bahaya yang dikenal.

Do I need to connect my wallet to scan?Harus connect wallet buat scan?

No. A scan only needs the token's address. Kaelis never asks for private keys or permission to move funds.Nggak. Scan cuma butuh alamat token. Kaelis gak pernah minta private key atau izin mindahin dana.

Can the AI be tricked into a good score?AI-nya bisa diakalin biar skornya bagus?

The AI doesn't make the score. Fixed rules do, and the AI's text is checked so it can't contain numbers that differ from the score.Yang bikin skor bukan AI, tapi aturan tetap. Teks dari AI juga dicek supaya gak ada angka yang beda dari skornya.

Which blockchain?Pakai blockchain apa?

The plan is Solana, because several checks (mint and freeze authority) are Solana features. This is not final yet.Rencananya Solana, karena beberapa cek (mint dan freeze authority) itu fitur Solana. Belum final.

How can someone fake Proof-of-Build?Gimana kalau orang curang di Proof-of-Build?

Only merged work on public repos counts, with daily caps, minimum account age, outside-contributor checks and manual review of the top earners each week.Cuma kerjaan yang di-merge di repo publik yang dihitung, ada batas harian, umur akun minimal, cek kontributor luar, dan review manual buat yang dapet paling banyak tiap minggu.

Is any of this live?Udah ada yang live?

Not yet. The website and Forge City run on example data. See the status above.Belum. Website dan Forge City masih pakai data contoh. Lihat status di atas.
