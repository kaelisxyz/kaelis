            How the score works

            Each check returns a severity from 0 to 1. Score = sum of weight × severity, capped at 100. Checks we can't read right now are shown as unavailable and add nothing. A low score means no known red flag was found. It is never a promise of safety, and nothing here is financial advice.

            CheckWeightRule
            Sell test25We ask Jupiter for a real quote to buy with 0.1 SOL and sell straight back. If selling fails or loses 50% or more, it's a honeypot and the score is forced to at least 85. If there is no route, 24h trade history is used instead.
            Liquidity depth15Full risk under $5k in pools, none above $250k (log scale).
            Top 10 holders15Share of supply in the 10 largest wallets, pools excluded. Linear from 30% to 80%.
            LP locked or burned15From RugCheck. Share of pool tokens not locked.
            Mint authority10Full risk if someone can still mint new tokens.
            Token extensions10Token-2022 controls: permanent delegate, non-transferable or frozen-by-default is full risk, a transfer hook is 70%, a transfer fee scales with its size.
            Pool age5Full risk under 24 hours, none after 30 days.
            Freeze authority5Full risk if someone can freeze holders' tokens.
            Wallet links (deep scan)evidenceFor the 10 largest wallets we look up the transaction that first funded each one. Wallets funded by the same source, or brand-new wallets holding a lot, are flagged with links to the transactions. Not added to the score yet.

            Kaelis Rating

            The score as a letter grade. It grades risk, it never says buy or sell. D is only given after the fact, when the Ledger confirms a rug.

            GradeScoreMeaning
            AAA · AA · A0–29No or very few known red flags. AAA and AA need every check readable: if one is unavailable, the best grade is A.
            BBB30–39Some flags, still low risk.
            BB · B40–69Speculative. Read the evidence.
            CCC · CC70–100High risk. A failed sell test is always CC.
            D–Rugged, confirmed by the Ledger 7 days later.

            Data sources: Solana RPC, Jupiter quotes, DexScreener and RugCheck. Quotes are only asked for, nothing is ever traded. No wallet, no sign-in. If the AI explanation is on, it only describes the checks and never changes the score.
