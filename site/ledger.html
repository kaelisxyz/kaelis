How it works

How Kaelis grades itself.

The callRug chance comes from the rule score through one fixed, published curve. A failed sell test is always 85% or more. One open call per token.
The checkSeven days later Kaelis reads DexScreener again. Rugged means no pool left, liquidity down by half or more, or price down 90% or more.
Kaelis IQA calibration score from 0 to 100, from the Brier score of all resolved calls. 100 is perfect. 0 is no better than saying 50% every time. Shown after 10 resolved calls.
Tamper-proofEach call's hash covers the one before it. Changing any old call breaks every hash after it, and the page checks the whole chain on every visit.
On-chainNext: the chain head gets posted to Solana as a memo, so anyone can prove when each call was made.
Not adviceA call is a risk estimate, never a buy or sell signal.
