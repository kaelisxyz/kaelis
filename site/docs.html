Overview

Two products on one public record.

What it isDeployer reputation and agent safety

Deployer reputation: a record for every wallet that launched a token Kaelis tested: how many tokens, how each ended after 7 days, who funded the wallet, which other deployers share that funder. Agent safety: one call an AI agent or bot makes before a trade, answered allow, warn or block.

Who it is forAnything that lets people buy a token
  • Launchpads, wallets and DEX aggregators that want to warn before a swap.
  • Telegram bots and trading agents that need a guard they can call.
  • Traders who want to know who is behind a launch.
  • Developers, who earn points for merged code.
Why it is hard to copyProof, not vibes

The rules are open, so anyone can reproduce a score. The record is not copyable: every call is hash-chained, written to Solana once a day and graded 7 days later, misses included. A copy of the code starts with no history.

How it works

Data, rating, proof, API.

1 · DataTest and trace

Kaelis tests new Solana tokens around the clock: a sell test, liquidity, holders, LP lock, authorities, extensions, pool age. For each one it records who launched it and who first funded that wallet.

2 · RatingToken, deployer, cluster

Each token gets a score and a grade from fixed, open rules. Each deployer gets a risk figure from how its tested tokens ended. Deployers funded by the same source are grouped as a cluster.

3 · ProofSealed and checked

Every call goes into a hash chain. The chain head is written to Solana once a day. Seven days later Kaelis checks the outcome and publishes its hits and its misses.

4 · APIRead by machines

Agents and apps read all of it: a pre-trade decision, a deployer record, the insider filings feed. Free while in beta.

Deployer reputation · collecting

A record for the wallet behind the token.

A token is new every time. The wallet that launches it, and the wallet that pays for that wallet, often are not. Kaelis keeps the record. Look up a wallet or a token →

Per walletTokens and outcomes

The tokens this wallet launched that Kaelis tested, each with its grade, its rug chance and its 7-day result. One risk figure from 0 to 100.

Per clusterShared funder

Deployers whose first funds came from the same wallet. A funder with 1,000 or more transactions is a hub (an exchange, a bridge, a bot service) and never forms a cluster.

LimitOnly tokens Kaelis has tested count. A wallet may have launched others that Kaelis never saw.
What to expectOn pump.fun most launches come from a fresh wallet, so one wallet often shows one token. Repeat launchers show up in the cluster. Tracing started on 12 November 2026 and works back through the Ledger.
LevelsSerial (three confirmed rugs, or two that are at least half of the resolved tokens), Flagged (one confirmed rug), Clean (two or more held, none rugged), Watch (no result yet, risk 50 or more), New (no result yet).
Not for saleKaelis does not sell ratings. Nobody can pay to change a record.
Agent safety + API

One call before the trade.

Kaelis is an MCP server. A trading bot or an AI agent (Claude, Cursor, Eliza, Solana Agent Kit) calls check_before_buy right before a swap and gets a decision it can act on.

Toolcheck_before_buy

mint, optional max_score (69) and warn_score (40). Returns decision, score, grade, reasons, the deployer's record and proof.

Tooldeployer_reputation

address or mint. Returns the wallet's tested tokens and outcomes, its risk, its funder and its cluster.

Toolscan_token

mint. The full result behind the decision: every check with its points and evidence.

blockA hard flag (for example a failed sell test, or a token copying a known asset), or a score above max_score.
warnA score at or above warn_score, a check that could not run, or a deployer with a confirmed rug.
allowNone of the above. It means no known red flag was found. It is not a promise of safety.

Add Kaelis to your MCP client:

{ "mcpServers": { "kaelis": { "type": "http", "url": "/mcp" } } }

Or call it directly (JSON-RPC over HTTP):

curl -s https://kaelis.si/mcp -H "content-type: application/json" -d '{
  "jsonrpc": "2.0", "id": 1, "method": "tools/call",
  "params": { "name": "check_before_buy", "arguments": { "mint": "<token mint>" } }
}'

01 · Check a token · Free

Check a token before you buy.

Kaelis runs eight checks, including a real sell test through Jupiter, looks for wallets funded by the same source, and gives a risk score.

  1. Copy the token's address.
  2. Paste it into the Kaelis Scanner.
  3. Get a score from 0 to 100, with proof for every warning.
  4. Optional: press "Watch this token" and Kaelis re-checks it every minute and warns you if liquidity is pulled.
0–39 low40–69 suspicious70+ dangerous

A low score means no known red flag was found. It is not a promise.

02 · Build · Live beta

Earn by shipping real code.

Proof-of-Build rewards merged work in public code repos, not hype. Your work becomes a tower in Forge City.

  1. Put your proof code in your GitHub bio and sign once with your wallet. No password, no OAuth.
  2. Ship. Only pull requests merged by someone else, into repos you don't own, count.
  3. Points add up every week and convert to $KAEL at launch. Your tower gains a floor.

Spam commits and copy-paste are filtered out.

03 · Check a wallet · Collecting

See who is behind a launch.

Kaelis records who launched each token it tests and who funded that wallet. One lookup shows the wallet's other tokens and how they ended.

  1. Copy a wallet address, or the address of a token.
  2. Paste it into Kaelis Deployers.
  3. Read its tested tokens, its risk, its funder and its cluster.

Counts only tokens Kaelis has tested. A short record is not a clean record.

Evolution · Patrol + Genome

Every generation smarter. Every upgrade proven.

Kaelis runs a loop by itself. Its rules are a genome, and a new generation is adopted only when it beats the current one on calls it never trained on.

01 · PatrolScan on its own

Every 30 minutes Kaelis scans new Solana tokens listed on DexScreener. No one has to ask.

02 · CallSeal a prediction

Each scan becomes a hash-chained call on the Ledger, with every check value sealed in.

03 · CheckSee what happened

Seven days later Kaelis reads the pools again and marks the call rugged or held.

04 · EvolveBreed a better rule set

Once a day: 600 mutants are tried on 70% of tokens, the best one is judged on the other 30%. It wins only if it is measurably better calibrated.

Guardrails: each weight stays between 2 and 40, weights add up to 100, the sell test never drops below 15, and every generation records its parent, its changes and its before and after IQ, hash-chained. Evolution starts after 60 resolved calls. See the family tree →

Markets · stocks + memes

Memes, rated like the S&P.

Kaelis rates tokenized stocks on Solana (xStocks: SPYx, QQQx, NVDAx, TSLAx, AAPLx) with the same rules it uses on memecoins, and tracks its best-rated memecoins next to the S&P 500.

01 · StocksSame rules

Patrol scans each official xStock once a week. Issuer controls (mint, freeze, permanent delegate) show up as risk: that is how a regulated tokenized stock works.

02 · FakesCopied tickers

A token that uses a stock's ticker but is not the official mint gets a hard flag (CC). Kaelis searches for them once a day.

03 · K-20Memes vs S&P

The 20 best-rated memecoins Kaelis has called, equal weight, rebalanced weekly, tracked every 30 minutes next to SPYx. Both start at 100.

04 · KIQKaelis's own ticker

The Kaelis IQ charted over time. It moves as calls are checked and jumps when a smarter generation is adopted.

K-20 is a benchmark, never an investable product. Nothing here is financial advice. Open Markets →

Filings · the SEC for memecoins

When insiders sell, Kaelis files it.

Company insiders must report their sales. Memecoin insiders never do. When Kaelis makes a call it records the token's 10 largest non-pool wallets, then checks them every 30 minutes for 7 days.

K-4Insider sale

An insider sold at least 20% of its bag and at least 0.5% of the supply.

K-8Liquidity pulled

The pools lost half their money or more since the call.

K-3Authority back on

Mint or freeze authority was switched back on.

ProofHash-chained

Each filing's hash covers the one before it, so none can be edited or removed later.

One RPC call per token per round (needs the Helius key on the server). Open Filings →

Rating + Ledger

A rating agency for memecoins that grades itself.

Stocks get ratings and analysts with a track record. Kaelis brings that to memecoins: every scan gets a grade, and every grade is a public call that Kaelis is scored on.

01Rate

The score becomes a grade from AAA to CC. A failed sell test is always CC.

02Call

Kaelis records the chance of a rug within 7 days. Calls are hash-chained, so none can be edited later.

03Check

Seven days later it reads the pools again. A confirmed rug turns the grade into D.

04Score itself

All results feed the Kaelis IQ, a public calibration score. See the Ledger.

A grade is a risk estimate, never buy or sell advice.

Full details

How each part works.

Live today: the Scanner with the Kaelis Rating, the Ledger and Proof-of-Build. Parts marked planned are what we are building toward.

Fig. 01 — Kaelis Core pipelineEach stage is logged and replayable
  1. 01
    Resolve the token

    Paste a mint address. Kaelis reads the mint, its pools and its largest holders.

  2. 02
    Run seven checks

    Authorities, liquidity lock, holder concentration, developer history, a simulated sell and funding clusters. Each check returns a severity from 0 to 1 with the transactions that prove it.

  3. 03
    Score 0 to 100

    Weights add up to 100. A failed sell simulation is a hard flag that forces the score to at least 85.

  4. 04
    Keep watching

    Scanned tokens are re-checked. If over half the liquidity leaves in one transaction, or the developer dumps, Kaelis marks a rug and the building falls in Forge City.

  1. 01
    Link your code account and a wallet

    Read-only sign-in to your code account, plus a signed message from your wallet. One code account maps to one wallet.

  2. 02
    Verify real work

    Only commits in public repos count, and only once they land in a merged pull request or on the default branch. Forks, generated files and copy-paste commits are filtered out.

  3. 03

    Each week, build points come from merged changes, review activity and how many other people use the repo. A daily cap stops anyone from farming points with tiny commits.

  4. 04

    A hash of the weekly results is posted on-chain. Builders claim their share of the Proof-of-Build pool with a Merkle proof, and their tower grows a floor.
