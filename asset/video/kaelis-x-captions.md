## Intro (10 s, `kaelis-intro.mp4`)

A token scanner sees one token, once. It has no memory of who launched it, and no one audits whether it was right.

Kaelis is built on the part that cannot be forked: a record.

Every call is sealed in a hash chain, the chain head is written to Solana once a day, and seven days later each call is graded in public. Misses are listed by name.

On top of that record sit two things: a reputation for the wallets that launch tokens, and a pre-trade check that AI agents call before they buy.

The trust layer for Solana. Proof, not vibes.

## Live (30 s, `kaelis-live.mp4`)

Kaelis is live.

What runs today:

▸ Calls. Every token it tests gets a probability: rugged within 7 days, or not. The call is sealed in a hash chain and the head is anchored to Solana once a day.

▸ Grading. Seven days on, the outcome is checked against the call. Hits, misses and false alarms are published, each wrong call by name.

▸ Deployers. Behind every token is a wallet, and behind that wallet is whoever funded it. Kaelis keeps both. Wallets with a shared funder resolve into a cluster.

▸ Agents. One MCP call before a swap: check_before_buy returns allow, warn or block, with the reasons and the hash of the sealed call.

The rules are open source, so anyone can reproduce a score. The record is the part you cannot fork.

Website: kaelis.si
Contract address: not live. There is no token yet. The track record ships first, and anything sold as $KAEL today is not Kaelis.
